Overview

Games: chess.com and Lichess accounts in this report. Excluded: copies of chess.com games imported into Lichess, games against the computer, games from a set position, and (for insights) games under 10 moves. Engine: Stockfish 19 on this laptop: every position at depth 14 with the top 2 moves, then every borderline position re-searched with 1 million nodes. Accuracy: Lichess's per-move formula, rescaled to chess.com's scale (fitted on your chess.com-reviewed games from 2022 on, where it lands within 3 points of chess.com's number in a typical game). "Similar players" compares you with your opponents in the same games, who were matched to your rating. Move classes follow WintrChess's rules (Book, forced moves, checkmate, engine's top move = Best, mate handling, Great and Brilliant checks), with the expected-score curve and cut-offs calibrated to 30 of your chess.com-reviewed games: like chess.com, the same slip counts for more when you're already worse and for less when you're winning. On games held out of the fit, 92% of chess.com's good moves stay good and 88% of its bad moves are flagged. Lichess opponents' countries come from their optional profile flag.
